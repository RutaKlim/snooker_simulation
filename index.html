Snooker simulation
Made with HTML Canvas and JS, to replicate a snooker simulation using real-life physics and maths.
Introduction

This project is a snooker simulation that is made using HTML Canvas and JS, as I wanted to implement real physics and expand develop my JS skills and my knowledge in OOP and general problem-solving within programming.

Throughout this, I will show the steps in order of what I implement, such as firstly making a ball move, accelerate, decelerate due to friction, cushion shots, collide with other balls etc.
